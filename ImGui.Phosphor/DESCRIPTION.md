A retro CRT theme and widget set for Dear ImGui, inspired by the ship computers of late-70s science fiction: green or amber phosphor on black glass, glowing text, vector lines, blocky readouts and a terminal that types its output a character at a time. Ships two themes, a scoped tint switch, a CRT overlay with scanlines, vignette and optional flicker, and widgets for frames, buttons, status lights, segment bars, readouts, alert banners, an oscilloscope, a radar sweep and a perspective grid.
