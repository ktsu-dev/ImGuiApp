Decodes, edits and writes images with no third-party dependency. PNG, JPEG, BMP and TGA are read into a tightly packed, straight-alpha RGBA8 buffer. The format is identified from the file's own bytes, never from its extension. The buffer offers pixel and row access, fill, crop, square crop, centred padding, and a Lanczos-3 resize on premultiplied alpha. A PNG encoder writes it back out with a choice of compression level and cleared transparent pixels. Caller buffers in BGRA, RGB, BGR or greyscale, with or without row padding, convert to the same layout. It has no UI or graphics dependency, so command-line tools and Dear ImGui applications share one implementation in place of SixLabors.ImageSharp.
